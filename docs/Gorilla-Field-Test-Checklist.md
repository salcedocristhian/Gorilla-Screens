## Before arriving

- [ ] Open the deployed app over HTTPS on the actual iPhone and confirm the logo, tool icons, maps and 3D libraries load.
- [ ] Confirm the device is not in private browsing and can save projects.
- [ ] Confirm photos/notes may be collected with the property owner's permission.

## At the site

- [ ] Tap **+ Proyecto desde fotos** and confirm **zero** initial openings and **no 22743 3D**.
- [ ] Enter the real address and client name.
- [ ] Capture overall front, individual openings with tape/laser measurements, fascia/roof and connection photos.
- [ ] Choose an opening photo, draw a Rectangular or Arch object and enter real dimensions.
- [ ] Add or remove one vertical/horizontal profile; select 1x2, 2x2, or 2x3 as physically intended.
- [ ] Confirm the opening's measurements **in the field** and tick verification.
- [ ] Try to approve with a required dimension missing; confirm the app blocks approval.
- [ ] If a screen door is selected, check nominal 36 x 80 clearance against the real measured opening.
- [ ] Approve Revision 1 and confirm only this project's own framing appears in 3D.
- [ ] If applicable, measure and approve the actual roof plan; do not choose roof type just to fill a blank.
- [ ] Review preliminary takeoff and inspect whether every piece is counted.
- [ ] Enter real unit prices in Precio.
- [ ] Export the 11 x 17 G-0 / S-1 / S-2 / S-3 PDF and review dimensions, photos and address.
- [ ] Export the backup JSON with photos to Files/iCloud Drive or company-approved location.

## Isolation test

- [ ] Create a second project; confirm its photo gallery and 3D are empty.
- [ ] Return to the first project; confirm its own photos and approved drawing persist.
- [ ] Restart Safari/PWA; reopen both projects and confirm no cross-project mixing.
