# Gorilla Screen Studio — Manufacturer Reference (V15)

## Source documents
- **Screen Tight — Screen Enclosures** (3 pages). See pages 1-3 for framework, hardware, fasteners, roof panels (solid aluminum), spline installation, product SKUs. Stored as `Screen_Enclosure_Instructions.pdf`.
- **EZ Screen Room** (28 pages). See pages 4-5 for kit contents, page 6 for channel orientation/door sequence, pages 18-26 for prehung door and frame examples. Stored as `EZ_Screen_Room_Installation.pdf`.

## Not interchangeable
Screen Tight and EZ Screen Room are **different systems**. Cross sections and part numbers are recorded under their own manufacturer. The drawn SVGs are **schematics, not actual shop drawings or structural approvals**. Neither file supplies a complete engineering design for user-selectable mesh roofs (flat/mansard/gable), structural member spans, local wind requirements, or the specified 7-inch super gutter. Specify these from actual manufacturer submittals and the engineer of record.

## Door sizes
The EZ manual says **36-inch installed width × 80-inch installed height** (p. 18) and **door leaf generally 35-inch × 79-inch** (p. 19). The example on pp. 22-24 uses columns cut 1 inch longer than door leaf and header 5 inches longer than door leaf width. These are examples of the EZ system, not general rules for other doors. The editor computes rectangular clearance beneath a theoretical semicircle but does not declare fabrication fit without field verification.

## Installation reference
Screen Tight advises .310 flat spline and top-sides-bottom sequence in p. 2; EZ advises alignment of spline channels and installing door frame before screen wall (p. 6). Installer sequence, clips, seals, anchors, and roof drainage require project-specific verification.

## Roofs
Screen Tight p. 2 discusses aluminum solid roof pans; this is not a specification for screen-mesh roofs. The stated slope 1/2 inch per foot is a suggested example for that context, not automatically a mandatory value for Flat/Mansard screen roofs.
